## Recording order
Record the story’s 24 character turns without an English narrator; preserve conversational timing. Record a separate clear rendition, the three optional practice prompts/models, four Booster scripts, then unused check-voice clips. Story has keys/door, delivery bell, unpacking and plate cues; no sound effect carries scored meaning by itself. Sofía is dry and decisive; Mateo is warm and enthusiastic. Establish actors’ actual speech variety rather than naming a synthetic voice a national accent.

## Model contamination and assembly
Listen/Drive use only the 24 story segments. Active uses the same story with optional P01/P02 after A09 and P03 after B15; models follow only response or skip. P01 and P02 need their own opt-in boundary breaks, not a long mid-scene worksheet. Do not concatenate the JSON array as an episode. Check and transfer recordings use different reviewed speakers and do not enter ordinary teaching queues. Verify an item has not been shown or modeled before scoring it as fresh.

## Song
Rewritten lyrics and two 700–900-character prompts live in the song source and production pack. Generate contrasting rap-pop and alternative R&B candidates when the music pipeline is available. Select by listening: immediate identity, hook recall, vocal charisma, audible words, natural Spanish stress and desire to replay. No full song was generated during this revision; neither treatment is selected or approved.

## Audio release checklist
Human reviewers listen to exact final words and prosody; compare generated Spanish with approved lyrics; resolve substitutions or add mapped exposure-only words. Measure runtime, segment boundaries, target timecodes and intelligibility. Confirm natural tengo que/voy a reductions remain interpretable; do not infer pronunciation from transcript accuracy. Obtain rights/provenance and user response-capture consent before app release. An old eight-second voice audition remains audition-only and is not reused as held-out evidence.
