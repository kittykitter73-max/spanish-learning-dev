# Borao certification and validation policy
Status: research-informed planning; no accreditation, official assessment authorization or external program validation has been obtained.

## Three different meanings of certification
| Route | What it verifies | Borao status and decision |
|---|---|---|
| Institution/program accreditation | A named organization reviews an eligible provider against its own standards | None. Instituto Cervantes SACIC is an accreditation system for Spanish-teaching centers, not an automatic seal for an app or a curriculum document. App-only eligibility has not been established; inquire before treating it as a viable route. |
| External learner proficiency certification | An authorized exam evaluates a person’s language competence | DELE and SIELE are relevant Spanish assessment routes. Taking our course does not itself award these credentials or authorize us to administer them. |
| Borao completion record | Our own declared activities or scoped abilities were completed/demonstrated | May be offered as an explicitly internal record; it is not accredited credit, official proficiency, or an externally awarded CEFR/ACTFL level. |

CEFR is a reference framework, not an automatic approval mechanism. The Council of Europe explicitly states that validating examination-to-CEFR links is not its role. Coverage language such as “A1-oriented functions” can guide internal design; a whole-level attainment claim requires adequately substantiated assessment across relevant skills. Our selective later B2-oriented tasks do not prove comprehensive B2 coverage or achievement.

## Current claims we can and cannot make
Allowed design description: “A music- and audio-led Spanish program designed around practical communication, retrieval and spaced practice.” Show specific capabilities and support levels actually observed.
Do not claim: “CEFR certified,” “ACTFL certified,” “Instituto Cervantes approved,” “scientifically proven Borao fluency,” a guaranteed time-to-fluency, or native-like proficiency from Core completion. Referencing a framework is not endorsement. A review conducted by ChatGPT is internal review, not review by native speakers or academic researchers.
ACTFL publishes specific usage and official-test rules. Keep our can-do text original, cite rather than reproduce framework materials, and do not issue official ACTFL scores or use their endorsement/branding without authorization. Review current permissions before commercial reproduction of their proprietary descriptors or materials.

## Recommended route
First produce externally reviewed content and a functioning pilot, then calibrate the scoped assessments and publish precise findings with limitations. Use independent learner exams later when learners want recognized credentials or when broader level claims need validation. Investigate provider accreditation only when the actual provider format and standards make it relevant; do not delay a small content pilot to pursue an unconfirmed app certification route.

Human review is a quality gate, not accreditation. A small pilot is feasibility evidence, not proof of causal efficacy. A completion badge is motivation, not a language credential. The program’s current certification status is none.
