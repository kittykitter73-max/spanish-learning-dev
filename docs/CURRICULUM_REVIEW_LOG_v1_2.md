# Borao curriculum revision and review log — v1.2 / content v0.3

## What was reviewed and what this means
Six successive internal review passes inspected the eight-stage master, all 48 unit cards and 192 unit targets, all 24 cross-course strands, all ten Core Arcs, the 100-concept seed, album/module plans and the first Arc’s scripts, lyrics, games, assessment and Boosters. Each lens below is a deliberate ChatGPT review perspective, not a statement that a teacher, native speaker, academic, customer or separate model participated. No learner trial, complete song audition, native approval, accreditation or efficacy study occurred.

Current authority: docs/BORAO_MASTER_CURRICULUM.md v1.2. The existing v1.1 master Google Doc and v0.1/v0.2 studio Docs are archived snapshots. The current sources and this log are committed together. The unit register preserves all 192 authoring targets; the coverage register preserves every seeded C001–C100 ID. These are authoring specifications, not live database migrations or a fully reconciled 192-to-100 semantic import crosswalk.

## Pass 1 — curriculum architecture and coverage
Question: Does every ability have an accountable home, and does the learner encounter one curriculum?
Finding: twelve seeded concepts were absent from the written Core map; the stage/unit language could still be mistaken for another progression gate. Four targets per card also sounded like four targets to learn at once.
Changes: complete 100-concept home register; explicit role distinctions for chunks, lexical scaffolds, constructions and receptive/intelligibility support; scoped critical functions and a new held-out scenario in every unit card; splits for overloaded units; authoring stages never unlock learners separately. Repair tools are available immediately rather than waiting for Arc 4. Arc 5 does not demand past-tense narration of ayer. Arc 10 is integration, not full conjugation and accent perfection.
Recheck: 48 unique unit cards, 192 target rows, 24 strands and 100 unique seed IDs are retained. Database membership and expanded-target semantic crosswalk remain app/import work.

## Pass 2 — Spanish teacher and native-language plausibility
Question: Is the Spanish accurate, pragmatically situated and something a person could mean?
Finding: early service requests and dame/dime needed context; perfect/preterite, probability and regional forms could be over-scored; some broad targets bundled several senses. The original episode’s premise lived in English narration and the promised changed plan lacked a payoff.
Changes: per-unit register, referent and meaning acceptance notes; distinct need/obligation/ability/permission senses; earlier personal-a noticing with later systematic treatment; conjectural deber without de accepted; regional tense and address alternatives preserved. Five-vowel, stress and r goals remain intelligibility-focused. New voices begin early. Core sounds use clean una/uña instead of an anatomy contrast.
Episode revision: Sofía wants a restaurant outing; Mateo has ordered a surprise meal. Delivery arrives, there is too much food and no table is ready; she helps and accepts a floor picnic, but keeps tomorrow’s outing. The final Quiero comer changes her choice. All 24 character turns form a coherent Spanish-only story. No narrator has to explain the plot for Listen mode. Three separate optional prompts replace nine embedded prompts. Supporting restaurant/plate/floor words are excluded from narrow scored checks unless taught.
Recheck: grammar and semantic continuity survive this internal text pass. Character humor, local word choice, final prosody and all actual pronunciation still require human review of exact text and audio. Simple graded speech is intentional; claims that it sounds like an authentic native podcast remain unproven.

## Pass 3 — varied learners and motivation
Question: Does the same system serve a returning learner, a true beginner, a hesitant speaker and a casual listener?
Finding: dense six-frame instruction, compulsory due review, repeated tests and long teaching episodes could drive learners away. A global inability label could punish someone who declines recording or encounters technical failure.
Changes: true beginners begin with want/obligation/plan in small encounters and add need/can/cannot later; all six remain the full Arc outcome. Strong learners may check first and reuse eligible evidence. Listen is uninterrupted and never demands a review or microphone. Active practice has deferral and short optional blocks. Two difficult attempts change mode or end that target; no weak-target trap. Fictional identities and situations are accepted. Written ability, spoken ability and receptive ability remain distinct records.
Recheck scenarios: a learner who knows the grammar but misses a new voice receives listening work; a learner who types accurately but declines recording continues with spoken ability unobserved; an anxious learner can hear a model without that practice becoming failure; a casual listener can keep enjoying music without fabricated mastery. These are desk simulations, not measured user responses.

## Pass 4 — academic and assessment validity
Question: Does each task support the precise claim we make from it?
Finding: fixed four-of-five/80-percent rules competed with six-function Arc readiness; a rehearsed response could be called independent or transfer; banks labeled B could still be primed by teaching or their paired listening answer.
Changes: removed universal total-score gates from the master; separate provisional routing, delayed retention and external proficiency. Elicited prompts are explicitly not spontaneous transfer. The Arc 1 source now contains 30 candidate A/B listening, production and manipulation items, plus two practical mission specifications. Production precedes matching models where possible; novelty filters span episodes, games, Boosters and checks. A pool label never establishes novelty. Alternatives may complete the task without proving the missing target. Low-confidence evaluation triggers a changed probe or review. One listening/one generated response per critical frame is a provisional minimum, not validated statistical reliability.
Recheck scenarios: “Necesito comer” does not prove C004 obligation in a contrast task; an accurate paraphrase may still accomplish a real mission. A model followed by repetition earns supported practice only. Choosing an answer earns recognition, not speaking. Exact B audio followed by identical B production cannot provide two fresh observations. A changed situation that is functionally identical to a memorized script does not establish broad transfer. Missing audio earns no listening evidence. Immediate Booster success cannot prove seven-day retention.
Residual: no inter-rater calibration, ASR false-rejection analysis or controlled music effect is available. Existing research supports design mechanisms, not our exact sequence, timing, thresholds or program outcomes.

## Pass 5 — music, business and production feasibility
Question: Would people choose this entertainment, and can we produce it sustainably?
Finding: generic English lyric glue, nearly identical hook delivery and mandatory asset counts threatened both distinctiveness and production cost. Unmeasured timings were easy to mistake for finished content length.
Changes: Ahora sí now uses a specific social scene, teasing voice notes and matching jackets, with three different melodic shapes for its core phrases. Two contrasting rap-pop/R&B prompts are 859 and 882 characters. A full exact line/concept map is authored; audio/timecodes/density are pending. No generic affirmation narration or grammar bridge is inside the song. Unit asset counts become coverage guidance, not a launch procurement quota. One story can support several units, but each target needs suitable evidence. Sequence is one working Arc, then Arcs 2–4, then remaining Core; later elective production follows demonstrated demand. All final duration claims require actual recordings.
Recheck: the lyric is more specific and performs a useful learning job on paper. No song was generated or listened to this turn, so melody, groove, charisma, accent and voluntary replay remain open. Previously discussed songs No Traduzco, Vámonos Ya, Qué Más Da and Mírame Ahora were not available as full lyrics/audio and are not falsely marked reviewed. Map/review them when supplied. The full later catalog remains commissioning briefs rather than fabricated finished songs.

## Pass 6 — consistency and adversarial review of the revisions
Question: Did the fixes create another hidden curriculum, contradictory timing or a false approval status?
Findings in the first revised draft: the package retained old 2–3 minute chapter estimates; a revised game still used an unexplained desire to wait; the worked master example was still called an episode; old studio segment links could be mistaken for the new scene; source array order could place every prompt after the story.
Corrections: replaced stale duration/route language, changed the game to a meal context, relabeled the master example a focused practice companion, rebuilt current recording sheets and explicit delivery modes, and required assembly from mode-specific metadata rather than JSON order. The episode has 24 story turns and three optional prompt/model pairs. Practice is not eligible as fresh readiness. Current index names v1.2/v0.3, the historic Docs, missing media and pending signatures. The old UX timing contract is identified as awaiting reconciliation; content handoff gives exact replacement timing without claiming the app changed.
Verification: JSON parses; source counts, unique IDs, prompt references, mode restrictions, all six frame coverage, model separation and lyric maps checked. No app/runtime tests were run because no app code or migrations changed. Machine checks verify the authoring contract, not pedagogy or native perception.

## What still needs external evidence
1. A qualified Spanish-as-a-foreign-language teacher reviews scope, prompts, alternatives and decisions on exact versions.
2. Native reviewers from intended speech communities check register, character plausibility and final sung/spoken performance. Several voices are needed; nationality alone is not assessment expertise.
3. Actual learners representing true beginners, returning learners and hesitant speakers try one functioning Arc. An exploratory 12–20-person pilot can reveal problems but cannot establish broad efficacy.
4. Human-rated samples calibrate evaluator agreement, false rejection, support dependence and delayed transfer. Fix task-level defects before changing global thresholds.
5. Final audio, rights, asset assembly and app evidence behavior pass their own release checks.

The review improves the current design and text. It does not supply any of these external signatures. Pending gates are named so the project can proceed without pretending they are complete.
