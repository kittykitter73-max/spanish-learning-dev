# Borao Core Arc Map v0.1

Core is a finite foundation. The learner should feel a clear sense of momentum toward unlocking elective modules.

The 100 Beginner 1 concepts are not presented as 100 lessons. They are grouped into functional Arcs. Grammar, pronunciation, and listening skills are woven into meaningful content rather than isolated as academic chapters.

## Arc 01 — Make Something Happen
**Can do:** say what I want, need, have to do, am going to do, can/cannot do.

Primary concepts:
- C001 quiero...
- C002 necesito...
- C004 tengo que...
- C006 voy a...
- C007 puedo...
- C008 no puedo...
- C090 infinitive chaining
- C089 high-frequency irregular present forms

Recycled/action vocabulary:
- C057 ir
- C059 hacer
- C075 comer
- C077 dormir
- C086 salir

Content jobs:
- anchor song: hook around quiero / tengo que / voy a
- spoken episode: conflicting plans
- Flip It game: want -> going to -> have to
- speaking mission: what do you actually need/want/have to do today?
- checkpoint: novel personal plan

## Core completion

Core is complete only when the learner has sufficient evidence across the major communicative targets.

Completion should require:
- listening/recognition on core chunks;
- retrieval of major high-frequency frames;
- manipulation of generative patterns;
- guided or independent production;
- at least some novel-context transfer.

Do not require every one of the 100 concepts to reach the same confidence threshold. Some are active production targets; some are noticing/listening support concepts.

Passing Core unlocks elective modules while Core concepts continue resurfacing through smart mixes and module content.
